# Local guide sources

Reviewed 3 September 2026 for the short “Local guide” list on the practical
information page. The page links to authoritative English-language references
instead of reproducing or maintaining a second tourist guide.

## Selected references

- **Visit Brasil — “8 Places to Be Enchanted by the Art Deco Collection in
  Goiânia”**: the official national tourism portal's English self-guided route,
  published 19 July 2024. It covers Praça Cívica, the clock tower, Grande Hotel,
  Goiânia Palace Hotel, the railway station, Teatro Goiânia, Lago das Rosas and
  the Pedro Ludovico Museum.
  https://visitbrasil.com/en/places-to-be-enchanted-by-the-art-deco-collection-in-goiania/
- **UFG International Relations — Guides for international students**: the
  official landing page for UFG's English practical guide. Linking the landing
  page lets UFG replace the PDF without leaving this site with a stale file URL.
  https://sri.ufg.br/p/13919-guides-for-international-students
- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Historic Centre of the Town of Goiás**:
  official English context and gallery for the Friday destination.
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/993/

# Local guide sources

Reviewed 3 September 2026 for the short “Local guide” list on the practical
information page. The page links to authoritative English-language references
instead of reproducing or maintaining a second tourist guide.

## Selected references

- **UFG International Relations — Guides for international students**: the
  official landing page for UFG's English practical guide. Linking the landing
  page lets UFG replace the PDF without leaving this site with a stale file URL.
  https://sri.ufg.br/p/13919-guides-for-international-students
- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Historic Centre of the Town of Goiás**:
  official English context and gallery for the Friday destination.
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/993/

## Withdrawn

- **Visit Brasil — “8 Places to Be Enchanted by the Art Deco Collection in
  Goiânia”** (published 19 July 2024) was listed until 3 September 2026 and was
  then removed at the organiser's instruction: the central district the route
  covers — Praça Cívica, the railway station, Teatro Goiânia and the rest — is
  not somewhere the workshop wants to send visiting participants on their own.
  The link is recorded here rather than on the page.
  https://visitbrasil.com/en/places-to-be-enchanted-by-the-art-deco-collection-in-goiania/

  Note that the reason is a safety judgement supplied by the organiser, not a
  sourced fact. If the site is ever to give participants guidance on where it
  is and is not sensible to walk, that guidance has to come from LAPIG in
  writing — see `research/pending-information.md`. Silence is the current
  position: the list simply does not recommend the area.

## Editorial decision

A short list of official references only. Workshop-specific weather advice,
field clothing, emergency contacts, nearby services and any local safety
guidance remain visibly pending until LAPIG supplies them; external tourism
copy cannot substitute for those operational facts.

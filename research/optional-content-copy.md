## 1. Time2Graze

**ID:** `about-time2graze`  
**Trigger:** About Time2Graze  
**Title:** Time2Graze: information for grazing decisions

Time2Graze brings together work on satellite observation and grazing decisions across Latin America and Africa. Funded by the Global Methane Hub, the project aims to help livestock producers understand pasture availability and use that information in their management decisions. [S01]

### From observation to decisions

Satellite observations, pasture models and information from users contribute to the development of decision-support tools. Research teams work with producers and local organisations to shape tools for different farming contexts. These are project aims and development activities, rather than a promise that every tool is already available. [S02, S03]

**Related:** LAPIG; Land & Carbon Lab.  
**Source links:** Global project overview [S01]; Tropical America work [S02]; Argentina and Uruguay work [S03].

**Visual brief:** A conceptual diagram, with four labelled elements: Satellite observations; Field observations and models; Producer input; Grazing decisions. Show feedback from users as a return connection. Do not label the diagram as a deployed architecture or show fabricated live measurements.

**Editorial boundary:** The Alliance page describes Colombia/Brazil; it must not be used to reduce the entire international initiative to those countries. GMH provides the broader scope, and INIA independently describes Argentina/Uruguay. Omit partner counts, methane-reduction percentages and performance targets: they are unnecessary to this introduction and could be misread as achieved results.

## 2. UFG

**ID:** `about-ufg`  
**Trigger:** About UFG  
**Title:** Federal University of Goiás

The Federal University of Goiás — Universidade Federal de Goiás, or UFG — was established in 1960 through the union of five existing colleges. Its activities include undergraduate and postgraduate education. [S04]

### The workshop's host university

The workshop is hosted at LAPIG on UFG's Campus Samambaia in Goiânia. The laboratory is part of the university's Institute of Socio-Environmental Studies, known as IESA. [Local workshop record; S05]

### Science and cultural life

UFG also participates in the co-organisation of FICA, the environmental film festival held in Cidade de Goiás. The festival offers another way to encounter the university's work beyond the laboratory. [S14]

**Related:** About LAPIG; FICA: cinema and the environment.  
**Source links:** UFG website [S04]; UFG and FICA [S14].

**Visual brief:** Campus panorama, avenue and library. Identify library photography as campus context, not the workshop building. Avoid university rankings, leadership names and changing student/course totals.

## 3. LAPIG

**ID:** `about-lapig`  
**Trigger:** About LAPIG  
**Title:** LAPIG: observing landscapes

LAPIG is a UFG laboratory working with remote sensing and geoprocessing. Established in 1994 and linked to IESA, it produces and organises geographic information for environmental and territorial monitoring. [S05]

### Pastures as a research focus

Its pasture research includes mapping and monitoring, with work on pasture condition and productivity. The laboratory's research programme provides an Atlas of Pastures and contributes to pasture mapping within MapBiomas. [S06]

### Explore the research

The laboratory's research pages introduce these projects and their data products, providing a useful starting point for readers interested in the scientific context of the workshop. [S06]

**Related:** UFG; Time2Graze; The Cerrado landscape.  
**Source links:** LAPIG website [S07]; Pasture Research Programme [S06].

**Visual brief:** Existing authorised building photograph plus research photographs if supplied. Link to the programme page for data products; resolve and check each product URL before creating separate actions. No imitation dashboard or invented biomass map.

**Naming note:** Current LAPIG homepage and UFG anniversary article use “Sensoriamento Remoto e Geoprocessamento”; other institutional pages still use “Processamento de Imagens e Geoprocessamento”. Keep LAPIG as the display name until the team supplies a preferred full English form.

## 5. Land & Carbon Lab

**ID:** `about-land-carbon-lab`  
**Trigger:** About Land & Carbon Lab  
**Title:** Land & Carbon Lab

Land & Carbon Lab develops geospatial information to help people understand landscapes and their changes. Convened by the World Resources Institute and the Bezos Earth Fund, it combines satellite information, scientific research and modelling to support decisions about land and nature. [S10]

Its work extends beyond forests to include grasslands, wetlands, croplands and urban areas. This wider view connects with questions about how grazing landscapes are monitored and managed. [S10; final sentence is editorial context]

**Related:** Time2Graze; LAPIG.

## 6. Goiânia

**ID:** `about-goiania`  
**Trigger:** History and photos of Goiânia  
**Title:** Goiânia: a planned capital

Goiânia began as a planned capital in 1933, near the older settlement of Campinas. Architect Attílio Corrêa Lima developed its urban plan. In 1937, the state government moved here from Cidade de Goiás. [S11, S12]

### Architecture and the city plan

Art Deco buildings and elements of the original street layout preserve part of that beginning. IPHAN recognised the city's architectural and urban ensemble in 2003; the listed group includes 22 public buildings and monuments. [S13]

### The city today

Photographs of parks, avenues and cultural buildings show different parts of the city that serves as the workshop's base. The free-time map on this page contains the places selected for participants. [Local page and organiser-supplied map]

**Related:** Cidade de Goiás; The Cerrado landscape.  
**Source links:** City history [S11]; Art Deco heritage [S13].

**Visual brief:** Short chronological sequence 1933 / 1937 / 2003, one photo-led urban chapter, and park photography. Historical and contemporary images must carry their dates. The same-view historical pair needed for a true before/after comparison has not been sourced.

**Editorial boundary:** This is cultural background. Do not restore the withdrawn self-guided downtown walk or infer independent-travel advice from heritage photographs. Do not call Goiânia the world's greenest city or reproduce unsupported rankings.

## 7. Cidade de Goiás

**ID:** `about-cidade-de-goias`  
**Trigger:** History and photos of Cidade de Goiás  
**Title:** Cidade de Goiás: landscape, streets and literature

Cidade de Goiás developed from the settlement of Sant'Anna in 1727 and later became known as Vila Boa. It was the state capital before the government moved to Goiânia in 1937. [S12]

### A town shaped by its setting

The historic centre follows the Rio Vermelho and the surrounding terrain. Its streets and buildings illustrate the development of a mining town using local materials and building techniques. UNESCO inscribed the historic centre on the World Heritage List in 2001. [S15]

### Cora Coralina and the house by the bridge

The Museu Casa de Cora Coralina preserves the poet's memory through manuscripts, photographs, correspondence, household objects and furniture. The museum opened in 1989, on the centenary of her birth. [S16]

### Cinema and environmental discussion

The city is also home to FICA, a festival that brings film and environmental questions into its cultural life. Its story continues in the separate FICA feature. [S14]

**Related:** FICA; The Cerrado landscape.  
**Source links:** UNESCO World Heritage [S15]; Museu Casa de Cora Coralina [S16]; UNESCO/NHK film [S20].

**Visual brief:** Street view, architecture detail, house/museum and a river view once identified. Preserve the existing single photograph placement when expanding the article. Do not label every museum or street a planned workshop stop.

**Editorial boundary:** No museum ticket prices, opening hours, independent itinerary or promise of an interior visit. No reproduced poems or invented English quotations attributed to Cora Coralina. Local Indigenous and Afro-Brazilian histories merit properly sourced treatment if added; avoid inventing them from a generic colonial narrative.

## 8. FICA

**ID:** `about-fica`  
**Trigger:** FICA: cinema and the environment  
**Title:** FICA: cinema and the environment

Since 1999, Cidade de Goiás has hosted the Festival Internacional de Cinema e Vídeo Ambiental, known as FICA. The festival brings together film screenings, discussions and cultural activities around environmental questions. [S14]

### The 2026 edition

The 27th edition ran from 16 to 21 June 2026. Its theme, “Água e Clima no Brasil das Nascentes”, focused on water, climate and Brazil's headwaters. UFG co-organised the festival with the Goiás State Secretariat of Culture, through Fundação RTVE. [S14, S17]

FICA's June dates are separate from the September workshop. This feature introduces the city's cultural life; it is not an item in the workshop programme.

**Source links:** FICA 2026 opening and context [S14]; UFG announcement [S17].

**Visual brief:** Festival venue, audience or outdoor screening with edition/year stated. Real poster only after reuse rights are established. No autoplay, film-strip ornament or stock audience presented as FICA.

**Editorial boundary:** FUNAPE and Fundação RTVE are different organisations. Do not transfer RTVE's documented festival role to FUNAPE. Translate the explanation, retaining the Portuguese proper festival title. Catalogue discovered at UFG is an external reading candidate, not a licence to extract film stills or posters. Do not offer films as streamable until official availability and subtitles are checked.

## 9. Cerrado

**ID:** `about-cerrado`  
**Trigger:** The Cerrado landscape  
**Title:** The Cerrado landscape

The Cerrado includes savanna and grassland landscapes in central Brazil. It supports a rich variety of plant and animal life and is an important setting for understanding land use in Goiás. [S18, S19]

### Seen from the ground

Photographs show the vegetation and terrain of named places in Goiás. They offer landscape context; they do not identify the workshop's farm or promise the conditions participants will see in September.

### Seen from space

A Landsat 9 image captured on 19 May 2025 shows Serra de Caldas and the surrounding landscape in Goiás. It offers a different scale of observation from a ground photograph. Serra de Caldas is a regional example, not the location of the Friday visit. [S19]

**Related:** LAPIG; Time2Graze.  
**Source links:** Cerrado at ICMBio [S18]; NASA image and explanation [S19].

**Visual brief:** Ground photograph of Serra Dourada and the satellite image of Serra de Caldas, separated and explicitly named. A short transition changes between views but never pretends they are a continuous zoom at one location. Avoid identifying crops, pasture health or grazing intensity in the satellite image without supporting data.

## What was deliberately left out

- **Prize totals.** No page consulted publishes a total fund, so the panel
  states the per-showcase values and nothing above them.
- **Currency conversion.** Prizes are written in reais because that is the
  currency they were announced in. An exchange rate is not a fact this site can
  hold: it would be stale before the workshop.
- **“One of Latin America's most traditional environmental film festivals.”**
  The prizes page says it. It is the festival describing itself, and the site
  does not carry advertising copy, its subject's own included.
- **Submission counts and jury names.** Not published on the pages consulted.
- **Anything in the future tense.** The 2026 edition is over. The panel is past
  tense throughout, and the opening says outright that the festival is held in
  June and is not part of the workshop programme.

## The Cora Coralina link

The prize for best feature in the international showcase is the Prêmio Cora
Coralina, and Cidade de Goiás is where Cora Coralina lived and wrote — the
house is now the Museu Casa de Cora Coralina, already covered in the town's own
story. The connection is stated in both the showcase board and a chapter, and
the panel's `related` list points at that story. It is a real relationship
between two subjects the site already carries, not a bridge built to have one.

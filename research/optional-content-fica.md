# FICA — sourced material for the panel

Rebuilt 10 September 2026, then rebuilt again the same day. The first rebuild
was assembled entirely from the festival's reporting of its 27th edition, and
the client read the result correctly: it was a page about the 27th FICA, not
about FICA. The second pass leads with the festival and keeps one edition, in
one labelled block, near the foot.

## What was deliberately left out

- **Prize values.** Removed on the client's instruction of 10 September 2026.
  The Prêmio Cora Coralina keeps its **name**, because the name is the point:
  it belongs to the poet whose house museum stands a few streets from the
  cinema where the prize is given. The Acari Passos and João Bennio prizes are
  not named at all — nothing consulted says who those figures were, and naming
  a prize without being able to say whose name it carries adds nothing.
- **The 2026 selection counts** — 38 films, seven countries, the per-showcase
  feature and short counts. All true, all facts about one edition. They were
  presented as the shape of the festival, which is what made the panel read as
  a page about 2026. The link to the selection article remains.
- **“Every June since 1999.”** Recent editions run in June, and the first ran
  in June 1999, but at least one edition — Secult's own announcement of a
  festival opening on 14 December — did not. The copy says *held in Cidade de
  Goiás since 1999* and *recent editions have run in June*, which is what the
  sources support.
- **The founding names.** Search results attribute the idea to Luiz Felipe
  Gabriel, Jaime Sautchuk, Adnair França and Luís Gonzaga, and the Goiânia city
  council page credits the state governor of the day. These cannot be
  reconciled from the pages consulted, so the panel says the festival was
  created in 1999 and names only the role that *is* documented in detail —
  Washington Novaes's.
- **Currency conversion**, and the festival's own line about being among Latin
  America's most traditional environmental film festivals. The first is not a
  fact this site can hold; the second is advertising copy, its subject's own
  included.
- **Anything in the future tense.** The 2026 edition is over. The panel is past
  tense throughout, and the masthead says outright that the festival is not
  part of the workshop programme.
